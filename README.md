## ✨ Features

- **Battery & Heat Optimized:** Adaptive background sleep throttling keeps your phone cool and fast.
- **`.mv` File Sharing:** Export and share encrypted `.mv` configs over WhatsApp or Telegram—tap any `.mv` file to open directly in MetroVPN.
- **QR Code Scan & Share:** Scan another MetroVPN QR code using your camera or gallery.
- **Live Data Quota:** View your exact **Used / Remaining GB** and days left directly inside the app.
- **Hotspot VPN Sharing (No Root):** Share your active VPN connection over Mobile Hotspot to a second phone or laptop.
